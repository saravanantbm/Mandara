WHY "MANDARA"

"Churn the ocean, and it will give you what you need."

In the old story, gods and demons wound a serpent around Mount Mandara and used it to churn the ocean of milk — patiently, together, through the difficult parts — until it yielded amrita, the nectar of life.

We kept the name because the mechanic matches the myth: something solid, turned in water, giving back exactly what the body was missing. No mountain required — just a cube and a bottle.
